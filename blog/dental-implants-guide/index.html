Dental Implants: What They Are, Benefits, and Who Can Get Them

A complete overview of dental implants at Dental Health Albania. What they are, why replacing missing teeth matters, the advantages, and whether you are a good candidate for implant treatment.

June 29, 2026Dr. Shukri Jahiu

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a biocompatible titanium structure designed to replace the root of a missing tooth. Once placed into the jawbone, it serves as a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or fixed prosthesis, restoring both function and aesthetics to your smile.

Why replace missing teeth?

Losing one or more teeth is not only an aesthetic issue but a functional one as well. Missing teeth make chewing difficult, which negatively affects the digestive process. Additionally, it can cause shifting of adjacent teeth, jawbone loss, bite problems, and speech difficulties. For this reason, replacing missing teeth is essential for maintaining oral health and overall well-being.

What are the advantages of dental implants?

Dental implants offer:

  • Natural aesthetics — a harmonious and natural-looking smile
  • Full functionality — complete chewing ability and clear speech
  • Bone preservation — maintains jawbone and facial structure
  • Stability and longevity — a durable, long-lasting solution
  • Confidence — increased self-esteem and quality of life

Who is a candidate for dental implants?

Almost any patient who has lost one or more teeth and is in good general health can be a candidate. Even patients with significant bone loss can be treated thanks to modern techniques such as zygomatic and pterygoid implants, which avoid the need for extensive bone grafting in many cases.

In cases of complete tooth loss, the most commonly used solutions are All-on-4 and All-on-6 concepts, which enable full-arch rehabilitation with a limited number of implants, providing a durable, functional, and aesthetic smile.
